# Current Context Pack

1. **What is the current goal?**
   Extend the Cognitive Foundations paper by expanding analysis beyond "reasoning elements" into additional behavior families that matter in multi-turn and agentic trajectories, and evaluate whether these behaviors predict success, robustness, and calibration across model families and capacities.

2. **What are the 1–2 behaviors under investigation?**
   Cross-turn information retention patterns (primary focus) and proactive information gathering (secondary focus, with operationalization contested).

3. **What is the biggest known risk?**
   Correlation ≠ causation risk: success-correlated behaviors are not automatically causal drivers, and linguistic markers can be stylistic rather than functional.

4. **What is unresolved?**
   Whether "under-utilization" is real or an artifact, whether proactive info gathering can be operationalized without intent inference, and what measurement substrate (linguistic vs structural signals) is best for agentic trajectories.

5. **What task is the RA being asked to do?**
   Help measure, operationalize, and evaluate cross-turn information retention patterns and proactive information gathering behaviors to determine their association with success in multi-turn and agentic settings.
