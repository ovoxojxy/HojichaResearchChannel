# Open Disagreements

## D1: Is “under-utilization” real or an artifact?
- Position A (RA1): models under-deploy behaviors correlated with success on ill-structured problems.
- Position B (RA2): correlation patterns may reflect annotation bias or uncaptured strategies; causality not established.
- What resolves it: controlled interventions + measurements that separate style from function.

## D2: Proactive info gathering — can we operationalize without intent inference?
- Position A (RA3): yes, via temporal patterns and tool-call structure.
- Position B (RA2): too subjective; weak observables; high risk of mislabeling.
- What resolves it: a rubric anchored to measurable features (time-to-use, tool diversity, horizon) + inter-annotator agreement.

## D3: Best measurement substrate for agentic trajectories
- Position A (RA3): structural signals (tool sequences/state transitions) are necessary for tool-using agents.
- Position B (implicit): linguistic markers are simpler but can be stylistic.
- What resolves it: combined model showing incremental value of structural features over linguistic-only baselines.
